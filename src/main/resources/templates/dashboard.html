UPI Offline Mesh Payment System

Create a UPI-like payment with no internet. The packet is signed (Ed25519) and encrypted (RSA-OAEP + AES-256-GCM), then flooded across a simulated device mesh. Until a bridge uploads it, the payment is an IOU — not settled. Two bridges may deliver the same packet; the ledger moves money at most once. This is a deferred-settlement simulation, not NPCI/UPI or Bluetooth.
